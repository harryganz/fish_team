# Head nouns that are not fish words.
#
# The matcher only ever compares a surname against the HEAD NOUN of a fish name
# ("largemouth bass" -> bass). A handful of ASFIS and Wikipedia entries are headed by a
# modifier, a colour or a body part rather than by a fish word ("White-eye", "Common
# silver belly", "Marine fishes NEI"), and those produce junk matches. Listed words are
# popped as if they were not there, so "Common silver belly" contributes no head noun.
#
# One word per line; blank lines and #-comments ignored. Matching is on the singularised,
# lowercased head. For a hyphenated head only the final segment is tested, so "eye"
# covers "white-eye" and "blue-eye".
